# Northfield Electronics replacement policy

> Fictional policy for the AccessDesk hackathon demo. It does not describe a
> real store, customer promise, warranty, or legal right.

## P-1: Damaged-item replacements

A customer may request a replacement when a product arrives physically damaged
or unusable. The request must identify the affected order and describe the
damage. Roman Urdu search terms: kharab, damage, toota, replacement, badalna.

## P-2: Seven-day reporting window

Delivery damage should be reported within seven calendar days of the recorded
delivery date. The delivery day is day zero. Roman Urdu search terms: saat din,
seven days, delivery ke baad.

## P-3: Product photo required

At least one clear photo of the damaged product is required before a replacement
request can be submitted. A PDF alone does not satisfy the photo requirement.
Roman Urdu search terms: tasveer, photo, image, saboot.

## P-4: Packaging photo recommended

A photo of the shipping box or packaging is recommended when it helps show
delivery damage, but it is not required. Roman Urdu search terms: dabba,
packaging, packing ki tasveer.

## P-5: Delivered orders only

The damaged-item replacement workflow applies only after the order status is
Delivered. Processing, shipped, or cancelled orders are not eligible for this
workflow. Roman Urdu search terms: deliver hua, mila, pohncha.

## P-6: One active case per issue

Only one active support case may exist for the same order and issue. A repeated
request returns the existing case and tracking number instead of creating a
duplicate. Roman Urdu search terms: dobara, pehle se case, same masla.

## P-7: Late requests need staff review

A request reported outside the seven-day window may still be submitted for
staff review. Submission does not promise approval or a replacement. Roman Urdu
search terms: late, der se, saat din ke baad.

## P-8: Staff make final decisions

AccessDesk agents prepare and explain requests but cannot approve, decline, or
issue a replacement. Authorized store staff make the final decision after
reviewing the statement, evidence, and policy. Roman Urdu search terms: staff
ka faisla, manzoori, approve.
